## The problem

A corner store or mini-market in Cuba sells in **two currencies at once**, with an exchange rate that changes almost daily, takes cash, Transfermóvil, EnZona or bank transfers, and is often **offline**. The usual tools are a notebook and a calculator:

- CUP prices are recalculated by hand every time the rate goes up.
- Change on mixed payments (dollars + pesos) is worked out mentally and money is lost.
- At closing time nobody knows how much should be in the drawer for each currency.
- Nobody knows which products are profitable or when to restock.

## The solution

**CajaFácil** runs on the phone or computer the business already owns:

- **Daily exchange rate with history**: every CUP price updates automatically, with a warning on sudden changes.
- **Point of sale** with search, barcode scanning (camera or USB), bulk sales and discounts.
- **Mixed payments** in USD/CUP cash, transfer, Transfermóvil or EnZona, with **change in the currency the customer asks for**. 80 mm PDF receipt or via WhatsApp.
- **Cash register** with opening float, cash in/out and **closing counted bill by bill** (including the 3 CUP note), with the difference per currency.
- **Inventory** with average cost, shrinkage and a stock ledger per product.
- **Reports** on sales, profit and margin, exportable to PDF and Excel.
- **PIN access** with roles: cashiers can't see profits.

## The result

A system that installs in minutes, **doesn't depend on the internet** and gives the owner what a notebook can't: a drawer balanced by currency, real profit and a restocking list. With several registers, they sync when online without losing a single sale.
