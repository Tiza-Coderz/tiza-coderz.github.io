## The problem

A business or creator posting on Instagram also wants to be on **YouTube Shorts** and **TikTok**, but doing it by hand means downloading, renaming, uploading and copying the caption three times per video. And from Cuba, Meta's developer platform is not available.

## The solution

**ReelBridge** is an automation service built with **n8n**:

1. The user shares the Reel with the **Telegram bot**.
2. The bot downloads it and shows it with the suggested title.
3. With **✅ Publish**, it uploads the video to **YouTube Shorts** on the user's channel.
4. It returns the **clean video and caption**, ready to post on TikTok in a minute.

Access is invite-only: each user requests access with `/start` and the admin approves it from Telegram.

## The result

A repetitive task done **from the phone in seconds**, designed for people with no technical background. It's a good example of what we offer in **AI automation**: connecting the tools a business already uses so they work on their own.
