## The problem

A designer's portfolio is her business card: it has to look **as good as her work**. Templates fall short, and a hand-coded site means calling a developer for every change.

## The solution

- **Static site with Astro**: loads fast even when full of images, videos and animations.
- **CMS (Sanity) that controls content and style**: any text, image or video can change its font, size, color and animation from the panel, with no code.
- **Video pipeline in Go** that optimizes videos and serves them from Cloudflare R2.
- In-house tool to extract design specs from Figma.
- **User manual** written for the designer.

## The result

A website that **looks the way she designs** and that she updates herself. Being static, hosting costs nothing and there's no server to maintain.
