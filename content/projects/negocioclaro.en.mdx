## The problem

A Cuban small-business owner gets paid and pays in **four currencies** (CUP cash, USD via Zelle, EUR via TropiPay, MLC via transfer), with an exchange rate that changes every day. With notebooks or Excel it's almost impossible to answer the basics:

- How much did I **really make** this month, in USD and in CUP?
- Where is my money going?
- Who owes me and which invoices are overdue?
- What do I give my accountant at month end?

## The solution

**NegocioClaro** answers those questions on one screen, from a phone, even on a poor connection:

- **Dashboard** with this month's net profit compared with last month, income, expenses, margin and cash flow. Totals in **USD or CUP** with one tap.
- **Rates by date**: every transaction is consolidated at its own day's rate. Fixing a rate recalculates the affected transactions.
- **Quick entry** with 3 fields to record a sale in 5 seconds; without signal, it's saved when the connection returns.
- **Receivables and payables** with due dates, partial payments and a **Remind via WhatsApp** button with the message already written.
- **Payroll** in CUP or USD with bonuses and deductions.
- **Monthly PDF report** and an **accountant-ready CSV** that opens correctly in Spanish Excel.

## The result

A tool that turns the mess of four currencies into **one clear number**: the real profit of the month. It's a PWA that installs on the home screen, loads about 175 KB at start and defers charts and PDF until they're needed.
