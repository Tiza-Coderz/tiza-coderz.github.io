## The problem

In a services market (repairs, maintenance, trades) customers don't know whom to call or whether the price is fair, and providers depend on word of mouth. **BYS** wanted a platform that connects both sides in an organized way, with payments inside the app.

## The solution

We built the platform's **mobile app** with Flutter:

- **Sign-up and login** with email (verified) or Google, plus password recovery.
- **Two profiles**: customer and provider, each with its own onboarding and screens.
- **Service requests** with map location, address search and photos.
- **Offers**: providers answer each request; the customer compares, accepts and **pays the offer** in the app.
- **Push notifications** and badges so no offer is missed.
- Feature-based architecture with **BLoC** and **Riverpod**, dependency injection and a typed HTTP client.

## The result

An app working against the platform's API in a distributed team: **feature branches, code review and hotfixes** without slowing development down. It's the kind of project that shows we can join a client's team and deliver at their pace.
